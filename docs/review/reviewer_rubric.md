# Reviewer Rubric

Reviewers should accept a label only when the evidence span supports the final label without relying on outside context.

Accept when:

- the source is registered or otherwise provenance-backed
- the evidence span is non-empty and specific
- the speaker role and section are plausible
- the final label is reviewer-selected, not copied from a machine suggestion
- contamination flags are resolved

Reject or defer when:

- the evidence is boilerplate safe-harbor, non-GAAP, operator, or transcript disclaimer text
- the candidate is an analyst-only question without management response
- guidance language lacks a period, metric/outlook, or prior/current comparator
- the source is external benchmark data, restricted vendor material, or weak-label-only
- provenance is missing

Gold labels remain transcript-first and human-adjudicated.
