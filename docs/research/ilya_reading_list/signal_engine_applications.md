# Signal Engine Applications

This document maps the list into practical future applications. These are candidate directions, not implemented product claims.

## Earnings Call Sentiment And Signal Extraction

- **The First Law of Complexodynamics**: Frame transcript signals as patterns between randomness and over-simple keyword rules Future idea: Compression ratio feature for prepared remarks vs Q&A.
- **The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks**: Motivates transcript-first sequence modeling before heavy architectures Future idea: Character/word baseline language model probes.
- **Understanding LSTM Networks**: Clarifies why long earnings calls need memory over many turns Future idea: Section memory features.
- **Recurrent Neural Network Regularization**: Warns that transcript models will overfit small corpora without disciplined regularization Future idea: Regularized local baseline checklist.
- **Keeping Neural Networks Simple by Minimizing the Description Length of the Weights**: Supports simple, auditable baselines before complex ML Future idea: Model complexity budget.
- **Pointer Networks**: Maps neatly to evidence-span selection where outputs point back to transcript positions Future idea: Pointer-style evidence selector.
- **Order Matters: Sequence to Sequence for Sets**: Important for transcript chunks: some order is meaningful, some candidate sets need order-invariant treatment Future idea: Permutation stress test for chunk rankers.
- **Neural Message Passing for Quantum Chemistry**: Maps to speaker-turn graphs, analyst/company interaction networks, and cross-call entity relations Future idea: Speaker-turn graph scaffold.
- **Attention Is All You Need**: Core mental model for retrieval, reranking, long-context transcript analysis, and future transformer baselines Future idea: Attention-inspired chunk reranker.
- **Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate**: Directly relevant to aligning extracted signals with source transcript evidence Future idea: Signal-to-evidence alignment report.
- **A Simple Neural Network Module for Relational Reasoning**: Useful for modeling relations across speaker turns, analyst questions, management answers, and entities Future idea: Speaker relation features.
- **Quantifying the Rise and Fall of Complexity in Closed Systems: The Coffee Automaton**: Suggests measuring information dynamics over an earnings call rather than treating all sections equally Future idea: Signal density curve.
- **A Tutorial Introduction to the Minimum Description Length Principle**: Provides evaluation philosophy for choosing simple explainable models until evidence justifies complexity Future idea: Model selection notes.
- **Machine Super Intelligence**: Useful mainly as a caution against overclaiming intelligence in Signal Engine 2.0 Future idea: Capability-claim checklist.
- **Kolmogorov Complexity and Algorithmic Randomness**: Deep foundation for compression-based novelty, boilerplate, and signal-density ideas Future idea: Boilerplate/compressibility research note.
