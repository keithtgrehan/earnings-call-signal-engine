# Initial Review Corpus

The first review pass should use five transcripts only:

- NVDA
- META
- NFLX
- SBUX
- FDX

This is a validation path for the review loop, not a benchmark-quality sample.

## Why These Five

The set is intended to cover different transcript shapes and signal-pressure patterns:

- NVDA: growth and capacity language, high expectation pressure, guidance sensitivity.
- META: cost discipline, platform investment, advertising demand, management reassurance.
- NFLX: subscriber, pricing, margin, and content-investment tradeoffs.
- SBUX: consumer demand, traffic, pricing, international exposure, labor and operational friction.
- FDX: macro demand, cost actions, capacity, logistics, and cyclical pressure.

## Expected Ambiguity Patterns

- guidance language that is directional but not explicitly comparable to prior guidance
- analyst pressure that is implied through follow-up structure rather than direct challenge
- management reassurance that may be boilerplate without a concrete commitment
- operational risk that may be temporary noise rather than persistent friction
- cross-section mismatch between prepared remarks and Q&A

## Review Goal

The goal is 30-50 reviewed calls after this five-call path proves the schema, export/import loop, provenance preservation, and evaluator reporting. Weak labels remain suggestions only.
