# Signal Engine Asset Strategy

The immediate path is not to train large models. It is to combine finance lexicons, SEC metadata, deterministic transcript labels, lexical retrieval, and explicit evaluation gates. After 30 transcripts, the registry supports weak-label audits and benchmark design. After 100 transcripts, it supports small supervised baselines and retrieval experiments. After 500 transcripts, it supports model-family comparisons, audio/prosody pilots, and multimodal ablations.

## Recommended Order

1. SEC metadata and Loughran-McDonald review.
2. Evidence-span labels and weak-label error analysis.
3. Financial PhraseBank/FinBERT as benchmark references only.
4. BM25/rapidfuzz/textstat deterministic helpers.
5. Small local embedding candidates after retrieval labels exist.
6. Audio/video assets only after legally safe media is available.
