# Portfolio Demo Walkthrough

1. Start with the problem: generic summaries are hard to trust without evidence and repeatable review.
2. Show intake/source discovery as the provenance layer, not as a label generator.
3. Show deterministic extraction as the canonical transcript-first path.
4. Open the review packet workflow and explain accept/reject/correct decisions.
5. Show `reports/evaluation_readiness.json` and `reports/source_quality_metric_comparison.md`.
6. Explain that TF-IDF/logistic regression and retrieval are support benchmarks only.
7. Close with the scaling step: complete the 100-call corpus and grow to 500-1,000 reviewed labels.

The useful product pattern is evidence-backed AI output plus measurable human review, not one-shot summarization.
