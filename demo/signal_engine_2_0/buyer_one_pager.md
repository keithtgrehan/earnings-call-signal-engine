# Signal Engine 2.0 Buyer Summary

## Problem

Teams already have transcripts, QA notes, CRM exports, and call recordings, but managers still lose time manually skimming conversations to find risk, coaching moments, and missed follow-up.

## What Signal Engine 2.0 does

Signal Engine 2.0 runs deterministic, transcript-first analysis across support, sales, and account-management conversations. It surfaces concrete signals, evidence snippets, and plain-English review stories without depending on LLMs or external APIs.

## Example signals

- Support: deflection, low directness, frustration, escalation risk, resolution clarity
- Sales: buyer intent, objections, pricing pressure, competitor mentions, next-step clarity
- Account management: churn risk, renewal concern, unresolved issues, commitment clarity, expansion opportunity

## Why deterministic matters

- every signal is tied to visible lexical rules and transcript evidence
- outputs are repeatable across runs
- reviewers can audit why a call was flagged
- teams can pilot quickly without adding black-box AI risk

## Pilot offer

- run on 50–500 historical conversations
- accept CSV or JSON export
- deliver a deterministic risk and signal report
- 1–2 week pilot
- no production integration required

## Ideal first users

- support QA leads who need faster review focus
- sales managers who want fast deal-risk readouts from call transcripts
- customer success and account-management leaders watching renewals and expansions
- recruiters and hiring managers evaluating product judgment, deterministic NLP design, and demo readiness
